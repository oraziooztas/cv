01 / proof of work

Projects I can defend line by line.

The flagship ships with its own audit trail: allow-listed bundle, SHA-256 manifest, independent regrade. Adjectives are cheap, manifests aren't.

agent-failure-eval-benchpublic · MIT

A compact failure suite for coding agents: 10 task specs, 3 executable fixtures with hidden checks, an F01–F13 failure taxonomy (F13 unscored in v0) and a grading rubric, with a controlled cross-model pilot and independent regrade that test the grading workflow. The three fixtures are disclosed examples rather than an unseen holdout, which is also why this is deliberately not a leaderboard. The craft on display is task design and benchmark hygiene, the part most evals get wrong.

dark-current-mlxpublic · MIT

Asks open-weight judges each pairwise question twice, with the answers swapped, on Apple Silicon. On a 12-item slice, three 7–8B judges gave order-dependent verdicts on 25–42% of items, unparsable outputs counted apart. Now the pilot of a jointly authored follow-up to LLM Judges Have Dark Current, at protocol stage.

LLM-as-a-judgeMLXpythonreproducibility
message-to-action-it

Turns messy Italian messages (WhatsApp, email) into structured action items: prioritised tasks, calendar events with ICS export and suggested replies, from a regex-based detector for seven Italian intent types.

typescriptitalian NLPautomation
sae-early-warningthesis · code private

MSc thesis research. Drafted a pre-registration comparing text, raw-probe and sparse-autoencoder signals as early warnings of multi-agent dialogue breakdown. On 700 generated dialogues the SAE arm warned about 4 turns later than a near-oracle text baseline (AUC 0.999): a negative result, from a setup that is still circular.

interpretabilitySAELenspre-registration
agent-eval-harnesspublic · MIT

Grades coding agents on hidden tests they never see, in a network-sandboxed copy, and stores the SHA-256 of the hidden set with every run. Stdlib-only runtime, CI green, with an importer for the suite above.

eval infrastructuresandboxingpython
iaa-kitpublic · MIT

Inter-annotator agreement in standard-library Python: Cohen's and Fleiss' kappa, Krippendorff's alpha (nominal and interval, missing data) and onset-window agreement for dialogue annotation. Reproduces 11 of 11 published alpha values on the MASC word-sense data within 0.001. Standard metrics implemented carefully, no new method.
